## Threshold outcome (pre-registered, `PREREG.md`)

> The consistency claim is supported for a dataset if **≥6 of its 10 best genomes reach full structural and compositional coverage (both 1.0) within the time box**. Claude-explained and Mike-verified counts are reported separately.

Full coverage = tool structural coverage 1.0, tool compositional coverage 1.0, and `check_explanation.py` `hierarchy.complete` (amendment of 2026-11-03), with the function preserved (max |Δ output| < 1e-9 on train). Time box: 3 h per network.

| Dataset | Claude-explained, full coverage within box | Mike-verified, full coverage | Threshold (≥6/10) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heart | 10/10 | 0/10 (0 verified so far) | met on Claude-explained count; not met on Mike-verified count |
| MONK-2 | 10/10 | 0/10 (0 verified so far) | met on Claude-explained count; not met on Mike-verified count |
| Adult | 10/10 | 0/10 (0 verified so far) | met on Claude-explained count; not met on Mike-verified count |

**Outcome, stated plainly.** On the Claude-explained count the consistency claim is **supported for all three pre-registered datasets** (Heart, MONK-2 and Adult: 30/30 networks reached full coverage within the 3-hour box). On the Mike-verified count it is **not yet established for any dataset**: Mike has verified 0 of the 30 networks so far, so the verified column is 0/10 everywhere. That is a statement about verification not yet done, not a failed verification. The pre-registered fallback wording ("Existence holds. Explainable networks arise often enough that a train-until-explainable loop is practical, at a median cost of X hours of training plus Y hours of explanation.") is not needed on the Claude-explained count.
