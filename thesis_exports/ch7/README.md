## Draft captions

### `ch7_table.tex`

The caption is in the file. Two points for the text: "Claude" means Claude-assisted explanation,
independently re-checked by script, and the "Mike" column (verified by the author) is 0/10
everywhere until verification is done.

### `structure_prereg`, `structure_supp`

**(prereg)** Structure of the best genome of each of the 10 seeds per dataset. Panels show:
- hidden nodes;
- enabled phenotype connections;
- depth (the longest input-to-output path, in edges);
- skip connections (enabled edges whose target depth exceeds the source depth by more than 1,
  not counting direct input-to-output edges);
- direct input-to-output edges;
- the structural monosemanticity proxy: the share of hidden nodes with at most two distinct
  original input features upstream. This is a structural proxy, not an annotation result.

Open diamonds mark the paper's network for that dataset, which is a reference, not one of the 10
seeds.

**(supp)** As the structure figure, for the additional analyses, which were not pre-registered:
Heart trained for 100 generations, and churn at 100 and 200 generations. There is no paper
network for these.

### `cost_prereg`, `cost_supp`

Cost per run. Left: training wall clock in hours (one process per seed, one CPU thread). Right:
Claude-assisted explanation wall clock in minutes, against a 3-hour box per network.

For the caption or the text:
- Adult training time is net of suspensions (`experiments/10seed/RUN_INCIDENTS.md`).
- Heart (40 generations) ran on slower code, so its time is not comparable with the others.
- Details: `experiments/10seed/figures/README.md`.

### `test_auc_prereg`, `test_auc_supp`

**(prereg)** Test AUC of the best (training-fitness) genome of each seed, with 95% paired
bootstrap CIs (10,000 resamples of the test set, seed 42). P marks the paper network.
- Dashed line: the paper's L2 logistic-regression test AUC on the same split (Heart 0.919,
  MONK-2 0.423, Adult 0.905).
- Dotted line (Adult only): gradient boosting, about 0.930. It comes from a PMLB 80:20 screen,
  not the paper split, so it is approximate.
- Heart's test set has n = 61, hence the wide CIs.

**(supp)** As the test-AUC figure, for the additional analyses (not pre-registered). For churn,
both baselines come from the same split: L2 logistic regression 0.813, gradient boosting 0.932.

### `coverage_time`

Coverage reached by each network against the time taken to explain it. Each dot is one network,
placed at its Claude explanation wall clock. A filled dot means full coverage:
- structural and compositional coverage are both 1.0, and
- a single root annotation, whose subgraph contains the output, has every other annotation
  beneath it.

A hollow dot would mean not explained within the budget; there are none. The dashed line is the
pre-registered 3-hour box. (a) The pre-registered datasets. (b) The additional analyses, which
were not pre-registered. All 60 networks reached full coverage within the box. These are
Claude-assisted explanations, re-checked independently by script. **None has been verified by
the author yet.**

### `xor_ridge_valley`

One signal, two families of realisations. Each panel shows the output score
z = v₁x₁ + v₂x₂ + c + u·ReLU(w₁x₁ + w₂x₂ + b) of a minimal network (one ReLU hidden unit plus
skip connections) that computes XOR on the four data points. The plots cover [−0.5, 1.5]²; the
outlined square is [0, 1]². Filled points have XOR = 1 and open points XOR = 0. Blue means z > 0
(predicted 1) and magenta z < 0. The solid line is the decision boundary z = 0.

- **(a, c) Count family** (hidden weights of the same sign, u < 0): the score is a ridge in
  x₁ + x₂, with 10 and 01 on the crest and 00 and 11 on either side.
- **(b, d) Difference family** (hidden weights of opposite sign, u > 0): the score is a valley
  in x₁ − x₂, with 00 and 11 in the trough.

(a, b) show the surfaces in 3D, with the plane z = 0 in grey. (c, d) show them from above; the
dashed line is the hidden unit's fold, w·x + b = 0. Both networks agree on the data and differ
everywhere between and beyond it.

Weights (w₁, w₂, b; v₁, v₂, c; u): count (4, 4, −6; 2, 2, −1; −4); difference
(−4, 4, −2; 2, −2, −1; 4).

### `xor_gates`

Each family of XOR realisations composes two logic gates. Rows show the count family (a–c) and
the difference family (d–f); networks as in the ridge-and-valley figure. Points are filled where
the panel's quantity is positive.

- **(a, d) The skips alone:** v₁x₁ + v₂x₂ + c. They compute OR (count) or x₁ ∧ ¬x₂ (difference),
  which is wrong on exactly one row.
- **(b, e) The hidden unit's contribution u·h:** green shading shows |u·h|, and the dashed line
  is the fold. Circled points are the rows whose sign it flips: exactly AND (count) or x₂ ∧ ¬x₁
  (difference).
- **(c, f) The composed score:** XOR = OR ∧ ¬AND (count), and
  XOR = (x₁ ∧ ¬x₂) ∨ (x₂ ∧ ¬x₁) (difference).

`experiments/xor/verify_xor.py` checks that the hidden unit flips exactly one row for every
sampled ReLU solution (12,000, 2,000 per cell). For sigmoid hidden units this two-gate reading
does not always hold.
